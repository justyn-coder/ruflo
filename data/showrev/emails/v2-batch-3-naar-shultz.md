## PROSPECT 1: Garth Naar — CEO, Avatar Tech LLC (Newark, NJ)

### Research Summary

- Avatar Tech LLC is a 30+ year fiber construction and design firm based in Newark, NJ. MBE-certified in NY, NJ, MD, DC. Federal contractor. Installs CAT6/CAT6A and fiber optic infrastructure across the Northeast corridor.
- Avatarius LLC is Naar's separate consulting firm that helps municipalities obtain federal broadband funding, then engineers, designs, and builds the municipal networks. Dual positioning: securing the money AND building the infrastructure.
- Columbia Engineering master's degree. Former Director of Engineering at Telcordia (Bell Labs subsidiary). 25+ years telecom consulting. Technical depth is real.

### Influence Pattern

Challenger insight. Naar operates on both sides of the BEAD pipeline (funding consulting + construction). The insight: the binding constraint is not securing BEAD dollars or fielding crews — it is whether permit drawings go in clean enough to avoid kickbacks that burn 3-6 weeks per cycle.
