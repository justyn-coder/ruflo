Conexon designed and manages the network build.

(2) As a co-op ISP, NEMEPA is the network OWNER, not the engineering firm. They outsource design to Conexon. Inorsa sells to the firms producing the drawings, not typically to the asset owner. (3) However, NEMEPA may be entering expansion phases (RDOF-funded extensions to additional substations, potential BEAD applications). If they are bringing design in-house or selecting new engineering partners for Phase 2+, the CTO would be involved. (4) Scale is modest: 29,000 total co-op members across 4 counties. (5) Mississippi's BEAD allocation could drive new expansion, but co-ops typically partner with firms like Conexon who handle the design.

**Path to PASS:** If Nathan is selecting engineering vendors for expansion phases and wants QC on incoming designs from Conexon or other contractors. Worth one qualifying touch.
