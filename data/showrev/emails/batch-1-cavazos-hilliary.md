## PROSPECT: Adam Cavazos -- Hilliary Communications

### Research Summary

- **Hilliary Communications** is a family-owned telecom provider headquartered in Lawton, OK. Founded 1958 (Medicine Park Telephone Company). Provides broadband, digital cable, and telephone service across Oklahoma, Texas, and Iowa.
- **Massive growth phase**: deploying $250M+ in new broadband infrastructure. Acquired TDS Telecom's Oklahoma operations (July 2025), adding 35,000 locations. Deploying 8,000-home FTTH system in Central Oklahoma. ARPA-funded projects totaling $65.9M across 11 Oklahoma counties.
- **BEAD pipeline**: Oklahoma received preliminary approval for $797M in BEAD funding; construction expected to begin early 2026 with services operational by end of 2028. Hilliary is a primary recipient.
- **Adam Cavazos** has been CTO since March 2019, promoted to CTO/SVP. 37-year telecom career starting at Dobson Communications at age 17. Oversees all network operations and engineering -- complete design, development, and approval of the network. Manages team for expansion, upgrades, monitoring, and maintenance across all Hilliary companies.
- **Booth context**: Adam mentioned doing long haul, short haul, some FTTH/FTTP. He referenced an Nvidia-based tool that seemed similar to Inorsa. The booth video caused some confusion about what Inorsa actually does -- T1 needs to clarify the product clearly.
