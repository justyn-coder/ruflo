## PROSPECT 1: Garth Naar — CEO, Avatar Tech LLC (Newark, NJ)

### Research Summary

- **Avatar Tech LLC** is a 30+ year fiber construction and design firm based in Newark, NJ. MBE-certified in NY, NJ, MD, DC. Federal contractor (enrolled in USFCR Simplified Acquisition Program). Installs CAT6/CAT6A and fiber optic infrastructure for commercial, government, and residential clients across the Northeast corridor.
- **Avatarius LLC** is Naar's separate consulting firm that helps municipalities obtain federal broadband funding, then engineers, designs, and builds the municipal networks. Dual positioning: securing the money AND building the infrastructure. Client base spans independent telcos, municipalities, competitive providers, and major carriers.
- **Columbia Engineering** master's degree. Former Director of Engineering at Telcordia (Bell Labs subsidiary) working on intelligent switches. 25+ years telecom consulting. Technical depth is real — not a finance-first CEO.
- **Newark broadband ecosystem** involvement: collaborated with City of Newark, Invest Newark, and Newark Alliance on Hope Village 2 broadband-connected transitional housing. Newark has its own municipal fiber initiative (Newark Fiber / GigXEro). Naar is embedded in the local government broadband pipeline.
- **BEAD dual exposure**: Avatarius helps municipalities secure BEAD funding. Avatar Tech builds the networks those funds pay for. He's on both sides of the 4-year BEAD deadline — the companies that survive it are the ones whose permit sets go in clean on the first pass.

### Influence Rationale

Challenger insight. Naar operates on both sides of the BEAD pipeline (funding consulting + construction). The insight he may not have framed for himself: the binding constraint isn't securing BEAD dollars or fielding crews — it's whether permit drawings go in clean enough to avoid kickbacks that burn 3-6 weeks per cycle. That's specific to his dual-entity world where Avatarius sells the timeline and Avatar Tech has to deliver it.

### Persona Bucket

**Fund/Capitalize** — CEO, capital allocation decisions. Sees the business from the funding-to-deployment arc.

### Approved Buyer Question

"Do issues show up after drawings are already produced, forcing redesigns?"
