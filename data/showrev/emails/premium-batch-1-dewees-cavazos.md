- Active ARPA-funded projects totaling $65.9M across 11 Oklahoma counties, plus an 8,000-home FTTH deployment in Central Oklahoma. Running long haul, short haul, and FTTH simultaneously - high volume, multi-type builds.
- Adam is CTO/SVP with a 37-year telecom career. Oversees all network operations and engineering: design, development, approval. He is the decision-maker for engineering tools.
- Booth context: Adam mentioned doing long haul and FTTH. Referenced an Nvidia-based tool that "seemed similar." The booth video caused confusion about what Inorsa actually does - T1 must create sharp separation.
- Strong ICP fit: multi-state builds, FTTH + long haul, high permit volume, scaling engineering team. Nick's qualifier pattern (high volume across jurisdictions) matches cleanly.
