## PROSPECT 1: Patrik Lowenborg - NetPMD Design & Integration

### Research Summary

- **Company**: NetPMD Design & Integration is a fiber network design and integration firm (HQ: Overland Park, KS) owned by The Pickstock Group / Planet-Air portfolio. Designs fiber networks end-to-end: planning, engineering, permitting, active network integration, handover.
- **Recent merger**: In March 2026, NetPMD Design & Integration merged with NetPMD Solutions -- this is the "acquired Solutions" the AE noted. Combined entity now covers full lifecycle from design through active integration. Chairman Mike Mason framed it as "accelerating continued growth."
- **Scale signals**: In 2025 alone, the design team completed 12.5 million feet of construction-ready network design. Completed "Project Santa" -- 70,000 homes designed in 20 days. Active projects include 900 miles of fiber in Escondido, CA (151,000 people).
- **BEAD involvement**: NetPMD actively supports BEAD applicants with feasibility design solutions. Expanding into middle-mile, long-haul, DC services, construction management, and as-builts for 2026.
- **Patrik's role**: VP Client Solutions & Principal. 25 years in telecom, deep FTTx/OSP/ISP expertise. Previously Deputy CTO at SiFi Networks. Manages strategic client relationships and tailored network solutions. Evaluates tools and software for design workflows.

### Prior Relationship Context

Inorsa has worked with NetPMD before. Price was the sticking point. NetPMD has since merged with NetPMD Solutions (March 2026) and is growing aggressively. The angle: their volume has changed, which changes the unit economics conversation. More volume = more ROI from automation.

### Research Summary

- **Company**: Booker Engineering is a telecom engineering firm (~26 employees) in Monroe, WA. Services: OSP construction prints, ADA ramp design, utility pole attachment permitting, traffic control plans, field surveys. Works with municipalities, state agencies, public utilities, and telecom providers across the Seattle metro and Pacific Northwest.
