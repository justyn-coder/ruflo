## Structural Differentiation

Each email uses a genuinely different approach:

1. **DeWees (B+T GRP):** Opens with the operational complexity of multi-state permitting, then bridges to his own stated request for a demo. Structure: operational reality, callback to booth conversation, product as solution.

2. **Cavazos (Hilliary):** Opens with the dual-workflow problem (long haul + FTTH), then corrects the Nvidia confusion from the booth without dwelling on it. Structure: pain diagnosis, misconception correction, product repositioned.

3. **Lowenborg (NetPMD):** Opens by acknowledging the prior failed conversation, then reframes the economics. No new pitch. Structure: shared history, changed math, invitation to re-evaluate. Shortest email of the six.

4. **Kariniemi (Booker):** Opens with a market event (BEAD allocation) and what it means competitively for small firms, then connects to his stated interest in technology. Structure: external catalyst, competitive implication, diagnostic question as CTA.

5. **Naar (Avatar Tech):** Opens by naming his dual-entity model and the tension between the two sides, surfacing a constraint he may not have framed this way. Structure: challenger insight about his own business model, product as resolution.

6. **Shultz (Ohio Gig):** Opens with the downstream consequence of permit delays (idle crews), which is the metric a Director of Field Engineering feels directly. Structure: field operations pain, scale multiplier, product as the upstream fix.
