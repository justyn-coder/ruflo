# Inorsa Fiber Product Intelligence Brief

## 1. What Inorsa Does (3 layers of specificity)

**For agents (use the layer that fits the prospect's sophistication):**

**Layer 1 — One sentence:** Inorsa automates the generation of construction and permit drawings from GIS/LLD inputs.

**Layer 2 — Technical:** Inorsa ingests GIS and LLD data and produces construction-grade AutoCAD drawings. The core value is SPEED — accelerating production so teams can take on more work and have more time for their own QC before submission.

**Layer 3 — Workflow-specific:** The manual process is: export from GIS, import to CAD, manually build drawings, submit for permit review, get kicked back, rework, resubmit. Inorsa replaces the manual GIS-to-CAD conversion step with automated output. Drafters then finish and stamp. The speed gain means more drawings per week, more time for QC, faster revenue.

**CRITICAL CORRECTION (Nick McManus, Dir. Customer Transformation, 2026-06-03):**
- Inorsa does NOT validate inputs or catch errors. Errors in the GIS data = errors in the output. Never claim "catches errors" or "reduces permit returns" or "quality control built in."
- Automation potential varies by prospect — depends on how well they leverage their network management tool. Do NOT promise a specific percentage without a file review.
- The "80% of drafting lift" claim from the sales deck is directional but Nick says giving a percentage without seeing the prospect's data is a mistake.
- The real value: Revenue Acceleration (faster), Revenue Generation (more volume), Opportunity (team freed for other work).

## 4. The Real Objection (from customer sales thread)

**Not "your product doesn't work." The objection is: "the OPEX hit impacts EBITDA."**

Customer VP of Engineering: "Our CEO/CFO have come to the unfortunate conclusion that the cost is too high and will have a direct impact on EBITDA since we can't capitalize any of it."

**Reality (confirmed by Tim/leadership):** Inorsa is SaaS, token-based. OPEX, not CAPEX. No seat licenses, no large upfront commitment. Tokens scale with volume — more LF = more tokens, per-unit cost drops at scale. One-time professional services for implementation are separate and not ARR. Tom's offer to restructure as professional services for CAPEX treatment was a one-off negotiation tactic, NOT a standard offering.

**Implication for outreach:** Don't promise CAPEX treatment. Instead, frame the OPEX as manageable: token-based pay-as-you-go pricing scaled to volume. The ROI argument is: "Yes it's OPEX. The throughput gain more than covers it — your cost per LF drops even after the token spend." For CFO-level prospects: lead with predictable per-LF cost, not accounting treatment.

## 7. Competitive Landscape (prospect-facing)

| Competitor | What they do | How Inorsa is different |
|-----------|-------------|----------------------|
| **Pivvot** (Terracon subsidiary) | Geospatial routing/siting + environmental compliance | Inorsa is DOWNSTREAM — Pivvot finds the route, Inorsa turns it into permit-ready drawings |
| **Render Networks** | Fiber network planning and design management | Planning layer, not drawing generation |
| **IQGeo / 3GIS** | GIS platforms for fiber network management | Inorsa INGESTS from these, doesn't replace them |
| **Katapult Pro** | Pole data collection and analysis | Utility pole focus, not construction drawings |
| **Biarri Networks** | Network optimization algorithms | Optimization, not documentation/drawings |

**Key positioning:** Inorsa is not a GIS replacement, not a planning tool, not a visualization platform. It's the step BETWEEN design (GIS) and construction (field).
