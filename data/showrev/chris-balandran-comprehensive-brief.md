## ShowRev ABM Strategy — For Chris Balandran

### The problem we're solving

Every fiber company at Fiber Connect got 50+ follow-up emails that week. Most sounded identical. Most got deleted.

We're building outreach for ~800 Inorsa prospects that doesn't sound like everyone else. Instead of flat emails linking to a product page, each prospect gets a personalized experience that teaches them something about their own business.

### What we built (click the prototypes)

**1. Workflow Assessment** — live prototype
https://showrev-microsites.vercel.app/assess/mountain-ltd

4 questions. 60 seconds. Prospect sees where their fiber drawing workflow sits vs. industry benchmarks. Questions validated by Nick McManus. Their answers pre-qualify them for the demo call — the AE knows their automation level, turnaround time, and rejection frequency before the first conversation.

**2. Engineering Workflow Schematic** — live prototype
https://showrev-microsites.vercel.app/pipeline/mountain-ltd

A technical diagram showing the prospect's drawing production pipeline — before and after automation. Personalized with their GIS tool, team size, and jurisdictions. Designed to look like an engineering workspace, not a marketing page.

**3. Industry Insights Briefing** (in development)

Curated data from fiber industry sources — Cartesian cost reports, FBA workforce studies, Pew research — personalized by the prospect's specific challenge. Not Inorsa marketing. Industry data.

### How they work together (3-touch sequence)

| Touch | Email does | Microsite does |
|-------|-----------|---------------|
| **T1** | Challenges an assumption about their workflow | Assessment → "see where you stand" |
| **T2** | References industry data relevant to their situation | Schematic → "see where Inorsa fits" |
| **T3** | Direct close | Booking link → "see this on your data" |

Each touch works alone (most prospects see one). Together they build a case.

### Why this is different

| Typical outreach | Our approach |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Static product page | Interactive assessment they engage with |
| About the vendor | About THEIR business |
| Same for everyone | Personalized by company, role, and challenge |
| Prospect learns nothing | Prospect benchmarks themselves vs. 300+ firms |
| AE goes in blind | AE knows their answers before the call |
