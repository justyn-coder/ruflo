## For Chris Balandran — ABM Strategy Overview

### What we built

Two personalized microsite experiences that prospects receive via email P.S. links, replacing the static "field brief" pages.

**1. Workflow Assessment** (live prototype: showrev-microsites.vercel.app/assess/mountain-ltd)

4 questions. 60 seconds. Prospect sees where their fiber drawing workflow sits vs. 300+ firms. Questions validated by Nick McManus.

- How do drawings go from GIS to CAD?
- How many unique CAD templates maintained?
- Typical turnaround to submitted package?
- How often are permits rejected first pass?

Output: personalized scorecard with their biggest opportunity highlighted. AE booking link at bottom.

**2. Industry Insights Briefing** (building now)

A curated page of what fiber industry leaders are saying about the prospect's specific challenge — sourced from 700+ podcast episodes, expert blogs, and BEAD data we've indexed.

If their challenge is "scaling without hiring" → they see quotes from leaders who faced the same problem. If it's "permit rejection cascading" → different quotes, different framing.

Not Inorsa's marketing. Industry voices.

### Why these approaches

| Old approach | New approach |
|---|---|
| Static page repeating the email | Interactive experience the prospect engages with |
| About Inorsa | About THEIR business |
| Same for every prospect | Personalized by company, role, and challenge |
| Prospect learns nothing new | Prospect learns where they stand vs. peers |

### What it means for Inorsa

1. **The assessment answers pre-qualify the prospect.** Before the demo call, the AE knows their automation level, template count, turnaround time, and rejection frequency. No discovery needed — they already told us.

2. **The industry briefing positions Inorsa as a knowledge source**, not just a vendor. The prospect shares the page internally because the content is useful regardless of Inorsa.

3. **Everything is measurable.** We track: who started the assessment, what they answered, who completed it, who clicked the booking link. The data feeds back into our system to improve the next batch.
