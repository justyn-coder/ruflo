### Common Pain Points by Segment

**A&E / Design Firms:**
1. Permit kickbacks from jurisdictional variation (every firm mentioned this)
2. Rework costs amplified by offshore delivery models (Centillion, IMMCO, Indus CAD Works)
3. Scaling CAD throughput without proportional headcount growth
4. Quality control across multiple offices/geographies (Mountain, Advanced 1)
5. Client retention risk when permits come back rejected

**Fiber Operators / ISPs:**
1. Multi-municipality permitting complexity (Ohio Gig, Schurz, TEC, LHTC)
2. BEAD timeline pressure (Avatar Tech, Ohio Gig, TEC)
3. Post-acquisition integration of disparate engineering standards (Hilliary/TDS, LHTC/PA Telephone, Schurz/6 ISPs)
4. Construction crews idle while waiting on engineering deliverables
5. Expansion into unfamiliar jurisdictions (FOS, LHTC, Mohawk)

**Construction Contractors:**
1. Crews and equipment idle when permits are delayed (Lighthouse, Rayco)
2. Multi-state permitting standards hitting project economics (Rayco: 5 states)
3. Drawing errors discovered in the field (JDI Fibertech splice mismatches)
4. Speed of engineering pipeline governing construction schedule

### What to Do Differently Next Time

1. **Capture AE notes for every scan.** Tier D (no notes, no grade) was 61 of 89 contacts. Research from scratch on each took 10-15 minutes. Even one sentence from the AE would have saved 50% of that time. Recommendation: require AE to dictate one sentence per scan.

2. **Grade at the booth.** Only 6 contacts had Hot/Warm/Cold grades. Grading forces the AE to make an instant judgment. Even "unsure" is more informative than blank.

3. **Flag vendors and non-ICP at scan time.** 22 of 89 contacts were equipment manufacturers, software vendors, staffing firms, educators, or other non-ICP. AE noted "hardware vendor" or "not ICP" for 4 of them. The other 18 required full research to determine. Recommendation: badge scan app should have a "vendor" checkbox.

4. **Separate partnership conversations from pipeline.** AirWorks, Biarri, Esri, Render Networks, and Dycom are all valuable contacts -- but they are not prospects. They should be routed to Inorsa leadership immediately, not processed through the AE pipeline. Recommendation: create a "Partnership" tag in the scan system.

5. **Prioritize multi-thread accounts.** Companies that sent 2-3 people to the booth (Lighthouse, LHTC, Advanced 1, Ohio Gig, Indus CAD Works, Rayco) are the highest-intent accounts. They should be processed first, not discovered during batch processing.

6. **Start research within 48 hours.** The show ended May 19. These emails were written May 28-29 (9-10 days post-show). For a 7-day show follow-up window, research needs to start the day after the show closes.

7. **Pre-build the industry intelligence KB before the show.** Having BEAD data, competitor intel, and segment analysis pre-loaded saved significant time during prospect research. Do this for every show.

8. **Track DNC list at scan time, not during processing.** 3 contacts were DNC. This was discovered during processing, wasting research time. Recommendation: DNC list loaded into badge scanner to flag at point of capture.
